**Test build.** LaküenRecomp v0.3.15 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It is the first build from
the resync branch, which brings larger blocks of the other Hookshot forks onto our base: smoother vegetation, the clustered
lights' own shadow maps, less CPU for locks and descriptors, a faster game build and a fix for the end of the game.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

**The shaders are prepared once again** after this update (the shadow-map change touches the shader translation), and the
game code is built again (new build flags for its cold parts).

## Smoother rendering
- **Vegetation keeps its passes:** grass, ferns and leaves are indirect mesh draws; each one ended its render pass for four
  barriers, a fill and a copy, and began it again. Now their arguments are converted together at the game's own barriers.
  `RX_VK_MESH_CONV=0`: as before. From Longshot (SadTransGirl, #1846), via HeyListen.
- **Clustered lights sample their own shadow maps:** the dark stair-step blocks on water and ice around the player and
  objects are gone. `RX_VK_HANDLE_TABLES=0`: as before. From Longshot #107 (pmwork-personal) and Lichii (Zortos), via
  HeyListen.
- **Less work per draw:** a pass keeps the targets its draws sampled as a mask instead of searching a list
  (`RX_VK_PASS_SAMPLE_MASK=0`), and a recording's extra descriptor pools are reset and kept instead of made and destroyed on
  the render thread every recording (`RX_VK_DESC_POOL_KEEP=0`). From Longshot (HumanBean, #1553) and HeyListen.
- **Capture buffers:** the driver's allocation no longer holds the free-list lock, and a refused one falls back to a spare
  or the exact size instead of failing the capture (`RX_VK_CAP_GET_FALLBACK=0`). From Longshot, via HeyListen.

## Stability
- **No freeze after the credits** under 60 fps or at an unlocked frame rate (the last movie's end is held until its skipped
  pictures are made up; 60 fps while a movie plays). `HOS_VIDEO_END_HOLD=0`, `HOS_VIDEO_UNLOCKED=1`: as before. From
  Project ReLink (pixl27).
- **A hang at boot on Linux:** a memory pool made gives its virtual core up while the renderer's pool locks may be held.
  `HOS_RELEASE_KEEP_CORE=1`: as before. From HeyListen.

## Faster build
- **The game code builds about 13-15 % faster** on the device: its cold parts use LLVM's fast instruction selector (the
  hot parts keep their flags and profile). `RX_COLD_FAST=0`: as before. From Project ReLink (pixl27).

## To try
- **Fast mutexes:** `HOS_MUTEX_FAST=1` keeps the game's locks in its own memory, one atomic operation each (ReLink measured
  the locks at 14 % of the frame's serial parts in a busy town). Off by default on Linux until it is tested on a handheld.
  From Project ReLink (pixl27).

## Kept
- The PC profile, Fog boxes (Off), Fog density and Brightness (100 %), the in-game diagnosis, the master log after every run,
  Update inside the builder, and the fixes of v0.3.8 to v0.3.14.

## Known issues
- Some places look too bright in a white haze (a fairy's fountain, the Zora's domain); the cause is open.
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds.
