LaküenRecomp v0.3.4 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It makes the builder bring a game up to
date reliably, brings back the play profile the game is compiled with, and adds the tools to track down the white haze some
places show.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## Builder
- **Update whenever your game is older than the builder:** the builder compares the version your game was built with to its
  own, whatever the update track says. Before, a game whose folder kept no game files, or a test build with the track on
  Release, showed **Play** and called the old version the latest. **Update** now builds the game again with this builder;
  your saves and settings stay.
- **No update track to choose:** the builder follows this fork's releases only; **Update** shows when there is one.
- **The play profile is back:** since NotRexed/Hookshot went offline, v0.3.3 compiled the game without the profile of where
  it spends its time (PGO), a little slower in play. It now comes from this fork's own copy.
- **The master log after every run**, on the desktop too and after a crash: the builder waits in the background for the game
  to end. **Open the master log** and **Create a report to send in** are on the main page as well.
- Test builds show as "v0.3.3 test 8 (43d38672)" in the builder and the in-game menu.

## For diagnosis (Graphics tab, under the Effect finder)
- **Create master log:** writes `masterlog-debugging.txt` beside `hookshot_log.txt` in the game folder at that moment: the
  settings, the light level of the last 32 frames, read-backs from the GPU that timed out, passes that read nothing, the
  Effect finder's ranking and changes, and your marks. One file to send in.
- **Mark a visual problem:** where something looks wrong, change it once: the log notes the moment and what the game did
  just before it.
- **Parallel recording** (As the profile, Off; after a restart): records the frames on one thread. The game's light level is
  a sum the GPU adds up each frame and the CPU reads back; Hookshot saw it come back as about 0, and every frame too bright,
  with parallel recording. Off shows whether the white haze goes with it.
- The master log gets a "visual problems" section with the marks, the light level and the read-back timeouts.

## Known issues
- Some places look far too bright in a white haze (a fairy's fountain, interiors around the market, the Zora's domain): the
  tools above are there to find the cause.
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds: the CPU records up to 2,800 draws a frame there.
- Rarely: glyph-like garbage in the dialogue history, black textures for a moment after the quick item selection (R1),
  shadows popping up with Shadow quality at Off.
