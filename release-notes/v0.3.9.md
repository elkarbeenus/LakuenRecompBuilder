LaküenRecomp v0.3.9 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It takes less CPU time per draw, keeps new
textures from staying black when an area loads, repairs more of a damaged dump and moves the GPU submit off the game's
thread on PCs with cores to spare.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## Performance
- **Less CPU per draw:** the replay's hot calls (descriptors, passes, the draw itself) take their thread's state once instead
  of at every use, a thread-local lookup each time. Measured on Linux: about 0.3 ms less a frame in the replay; upstream saw
  0.5 to 1.2 ms on a Steam Deck. From Project ReLink #89 and upstream.
- **Mesa's submit thread on PCs with 12 hardware threads or more** (Linux, AMD): the kernel submit leaves the thread that
  replays the frame. Not on the Steam Deck or the Legion Go S (8 threads), where the extra thread would take a core the game
  needs. `RX_VK_RADV_SUBMIT_THREAD=1`: on anywhere, `=0`: off. From Project ReLink #86, with HeyListen's 12-thread rule.

## Fewer black textures at area loads
- The upload budget that spreads an area's new textures over a few frames now counts each thread's own upload time, not all
  threads' together: entering a town from the field, HeyListen measured 470 new textures held back (drawn black for a moment)
  before and 116 after. `RX_VK_UPLOAD_BUDGET_SUM=1`: as before. From HeyListen (Mooseca).

## Dump repair
- **Every damaged texture of a dump is found now**, and each one with an intact relative gets a stand-in (the same texture of
  the other age, or the nearest one of the same size and kind). A damaged piece of an area's baked lighting had left what it
  lights black (black cliffs, missing rocks). Still made from your own files at the build, your dump is not changed
  (`mods/00-dump-repair`, with a README of what it stands in for). From Project ReLink.

## Kept
- The PC profile, Fog boxes (Off), Fog density and Brightness (100 %), the in-game diagnosis, the master log after every run,
  Update inside the builder, v0.3.8's Game Mode, memory and pipeline fixes.

## Known issues
- Some places look too bright in a white haze (a fairy's fountain, the Zora's domain); the cause is open.
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds.
