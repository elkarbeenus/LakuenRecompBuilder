LaküenRecomp v0.3.6 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It is a fix for v0.3.5's frame rate.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## Fixed
- **The frame rate on Linux with AMD (RADV) is back to v0.3.4's.** v0.3.5 made a readback of the light meter wait for its
  write-back on RADV; there the waits ran out (76 timeouts in 1,350 frames on an RX 6600 XT), each a stall. That wait is
  off again (`RX_VK_READBACK_STRICT=1` turns it on, for tests).

## Kept from v0.3.5
- **Fog boxes** (Graphics tab, Off by default): two fog-volume draws that come out wrong are left out.
- Fog density and Brightness, the in-game diagnosis, the master log after every run.

## Known issues
- Some places still look too bright in a white haze (a fairy's fountain, the Zora's domain): Fog boxes Off did not remove
  it; Fog density and Brightness soften it. The cause is open.
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds.
