LaküenRecomp v0.3.5 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It takes on the white haze and the light
pulsing from two other Hookshot forks.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## Against the white haze
- **Fog boxes** (Graphics tab, Off by default, at once): two small draws that read the fog volumes into a half-size HDR
  target every frame come out wrong. Project ReLink found them making figures white in water and fountains, and hanging
  some GPUs there; without them the rest of the picture is the same. They are left out now; **On** draws them as the game
  asks (`RX_VK_FOG_BOXES=1`: always). The Effect finder could not find them: too cheap to rank.
- **The light level read after it lands, on RADV** (Linux with AMD: the Steam Deck, the Legion Go S, Radeon PCs): there the
  GPU reports its work done before the write-back of the light meter's sums has arrived, and the auto-exposure jumped
  8-15 % brighter for a moment. A readback now waits for the write-back itself (from HeyListen; `RX_VK_READBACK_STRICT=0`
  turns it off, `=1` on any driver).
- Fog density and Brightness (v0.3.4) stay for what is left.

## Also
- HDR ASTC textures are uploaded from memory even where render targets used their storage before, as the other
  block-compressed formats already were (from Project ReLink).
- One mark each 2 s at most with **Mark a visual problem** (a held key made 80).

## Known issues
- Some places may still look too bright (a fairy's fountain, the Zora's domain): please tell whether **Fog boxes** Off
  helped, and send `masterlog-debugging.txt` (Create master log) where it did not.
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds.
