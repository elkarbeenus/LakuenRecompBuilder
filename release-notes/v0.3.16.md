LaküenRecomp v0.3.16 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It continues from v0.3.14 (the resync test
build v0.3.15 is left aside): a fix attempt for the white haze, fewer hitches on 4- and 6-core handhelds, less memory, and two
stability fixes.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

Coming from v0.3.14 the shaders are **not** prepared again. Coming from the v0.3.15 test build they are, once.

## The white haze
- **The exposure is no longer held from the area before:** the game reads its light meter from three blocks every frame, and
  each one is written only where its program runs. Since v0.3.12 a zero read got the block's last sums again, also once its
  program had stopped, so a place entered from a brighter or darker one could keep that place's light reading. Now a zero
  read is held only while the block's program is still running; after that its zeros are the game's, as on the console.
  This is our best lead for the haze in some areas and rooms; please tell us whether it is gone. `RX_VK_METER_HOLD=2`: as
  before. Built on HeyListen's map of the meter.
- The log now says, every 2000 reads, how often the meter's programs ran between the game's reads. If the haze stays, a log
  from such a place helps. To try: `RX_VK_METER_ZERO=2` clears a block only once between reads.

## Smoother
- **Background threads give way to the frame:** decompression and the area preload take a core only while fewer than the cap
  are held, and give it up when a frame thread needs it. Automatic: on 4- and 6-core handhelds and PCs; nothing changes on 8
  cores and more. Project ReLink measured 52.3 -> 56.7 fps in the Lost Woods on 6 cores; HeyListen 8-30 % fewer frames over
  25 ms. `HOS_BG_CAP=0`: as before. From Project ReLink (pixl27), via HeyListen.
- **Texture reads and writes that wait for the GPU** no longer hold a game thread's core. From Project ReLink (pixl27).

## Less memory
- **0.3-0.6 GB less** after the shader preparation: the compute programs' shader modules are let go once their pipelines are
  made. `RX_VK_KEEP_MODULES=1`: kept. From Project ReLink (pixl27).

## Stability
- **No freeze after the credits** under 60 fps or at an unlocked frame rate. `HOS_VIDEO_END_HOLD=0`, `HOS_VIDEO_UNLOCKED=1`: as
  before. From Project ReLink (pixl27).
- **A hang at boot on Linux:** a memory pool made gives its virtual core up while the renderer's pool locks may be held.
  `HOS_RELEASE_KEEP_CORE=1`: as before. From HeyListen.

## Kept
- The PC profile, Fog boxes (Off), Fog density and Brightness (100 %), the in-game diagnosis, the master log after every run,
  Update inside the builder, and the fixes of v0.3.8 to v0.3.14.

## Known issues
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds.
