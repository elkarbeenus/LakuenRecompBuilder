LaküenRecomp v0.3.11 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It keeps the first start's pipeline
prediction from running a handheld out of memory, and the Scarecrow's Song works above 60 fps.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## Fixed
- **The first start's pipeline prediction holds back on memory:** after the shaders are prepared, the prediction builds the
  pipelines a draw could ask for, for the driver's own cache. Two of the game's programs (502k and 1014k words of SPIR-V)
  each kept the driver's compiler busy for minutes and past 11 GB of memory on a PC; every other program takes seconds and a
  few hundred MB. Programs over 300k words are now left to the draw that needs them, and the prediction stops, the rest left
  to play, when the system has less than 2 GB available. `RX_VK_PREDICT_MAX_KWORDS=0` / `RX_VK_PREDICT_RESERVE_MB=0`: no
  limit. From HeyListen.
- **Scarecrow's Song above 60 fps:** its playback, the eight-note recording and its completion count frames in whole steps,
  which above a 60 fps cap were 0 every frame: playback stayed on its first note and a recording lost its rhythm. They now
  keep the fractions, as the other counters fixed before. A song recorded above 60 fps before this release: record it again.
  From HeyListen, after upstream Hookshot and Longshot.

## Kept
- The PC profile, Fog boxes (Off), Fog density and Brightness (100 %), the in-game diagnosis, the master log after every run,
  Update inside the builder, the fixes of v0.3.8 to v0.3.10. The prepared shaders stay: nothing here changes the shader
  translation.

## Known issues
- Some places look too bright in a white haze (a fairy's fountain, the Zora's domain); the cause is open.
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds.
