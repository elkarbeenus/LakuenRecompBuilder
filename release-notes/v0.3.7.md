LaküenRecomp v0.3.7 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It is about performance: shorter hitches at
area changes and loads, and less CPU work where a damaged texture made the game reload a model for ever.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## Performance
- **Damaged textures repaired at every build:** some dumps hold textures whose first kilobytes are zeros. The game cannot
  decode them and loads the model that needs one again and again, 12 to 22 times a second, more than a CPU core in play
  (as adult Link nearly everywhere), and the fairy fountains' floor and walls were not drawn. The builder now writes a
  small mod with stand-ins made from your own files (`mods/00-dump-repair`; your dump is not changed). From Project ReLink.
- **Shorter hitches when the GPU is busy:** the game's wait for the GPU in a capture gives up after a limit that follows
  your GPU (20 ms or four times its usual long wait, at most 250 ms as before). Arriving in a busy area, the GPU uploading
  its textures, the game no longer stands still for 140-220 ms. Project ReLink measured about a fifth less time lost in
  long frames; the adaptive limit keeps slower GPUs from flashing white. `RX_VK_CAPTURE_WAIT_MAX_MS=0`: as before.
- **Loads on less CPU (Steam Deck, Legion Go S profiles):** the game's .zs files are decoded with the PC's own libzstd
  instead of the recompiled decoder (`HOS_NATIVE_ZSTD=1`, upstream's switch, now on in the handheld profiles). A handheld
  profile already applied gets the switch by itself, your settings stay as they are. The PC profile is unchanged.

## Fixed
- v0.3.5's frame-rate drop on Linux with AMD (RADV): its readback wait stays off (v0.3.6).

## Kept
- Fog boxes (Off), Fog density and Brightness (100 %), the in-game diagnosis, the master log after every run, Update
  inside the builder.

## Known issues
- Some places look too bright in a white haze (a fairy's fountain, the Zora's domain); the cause is open.
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds.
