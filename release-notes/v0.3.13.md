LaküenRecomp v0.3.13 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It brings three of Longshot's fixes: the
game's clocks keep their pace below 60 fps, the market stops reloading its neighbouring maps as Link turns, and fewer
hitches at loads.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## Below 60 fps (handhelds)
- **The game's clocks keep their pace:** with Game speed Real time, at the 60 fps cap and a frame rate under 60 that is not
  30, 20 or 15, the game steps 60 / rate a frame (1.30 at 46 fps), and its cuts to whole frames dropped the fraction: 46
  frames counted 46 whole frames, not 60. The time of day, the floor web and the Scarecrow's Song ran at 77 %. Those counters
  now keep their fractions, as they already did above a 60 cap. `HOS_FRAME_CARRY=0`: as before.

## Fewer hitches
- **The neighbouring map's preload holds its choice:** in places like Castle Town's market Link stands within reach of two
  neighbouring maps, and the game's preload switched between them as he turned; each switch cancelled one map's loading and
  started the other from the start (a background core busy with loading for nothing). A new choice now counts once it has
  stayed the choice for 2 seconds. `HOS_OCB_HOLD_MS=0`: the game's own choice every frame.
- **Capture buffers freed on a thread of their own:** freeing a large capture buffer (64 to 256 MB) is the driver unmapping
  every page, and it ran in the middle of a frame at loads, 25 to 49 ms in Longshot's measurements. It now runs on a thread of
  its own. `RX_VK_CAP_FREE_ASYNC=0`: as before.

## Kept
- The PC profile, Fog boxes (Off), Fog density and Brightness (100 %), the in-game diagnosis, the master log after every run,
  Update inside the builder, the fixes of v0.3.8 to v0.3.12. The prepared shaders stay: nothing here changes the shader
  translation. The game's code is compiled again in one small place (the preload's choice).

## Known issues
- Some places look too bright in a white haze (a fairy's fountain, the Zora's domain); the cause is open.
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds.
