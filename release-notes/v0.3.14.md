LaküenRecomp v0.3.14 is one build for a PC, the Steam Deck and the Legion Go S. It takes CPU time out of the game's thread
handling and a source of hitches out of the render thread: six fixes from Longshot, upstream Hookshot and Project ReLink.

This release contains no game data, keys or executables: the AppImage is the builder, which builds the game on your PC from
your own copy. All of the recompilation work is NotRexed's and the Hookshot contributors'.

## Less CPU for the game's threads (most on handhelds)
- **The game's yield keeps the processor:** the game's job system yields after every job and every actor callback. Each
  yield handed the game's virtual core to a waiting thread and also asked the OS to switch threads; on a PC of 4 cores the
  job threads spent about half their CPU time in that switch, and frames were about 2 % shorter without it. Now the virtual
  core is handed over and the processor stays. `HOS_OS_YIELD=1`: as before. From Longshot (SadTransGirl, #1663).
- **A yield with nobody waiting takes no lock:** a thread that holds a virtual core with nobody queued returns from its
  yield at once. `HOS_VC_YIELD_FAST=0`: as before. From Longshot (HumanBean, #1553).
- **A wake with nobody asleep makes no system call:** the host's condition variables count their sleepers, and a wake that
  finds none returns without the futex call. `HOS_CV_WAKE_SKIP=0`: as before. From Longshot (HumanBean, #1553).
- **A freed region's discard gives the virtual core up** while the kernel discards it (up to 140 ms at area changes), so
  another game thread runs meanwhile. From upstream Hookshot (#2924).
- **The sync-object table's rehash reads a byte a slot,** not every object (62.9 ms to 6.85 ms at 524,288 slots upstream).
  `HOS_SYNC_REHASH_OLD=1`: as before. From Project ReLink and upstream Hookshot.

## Fewer hitches
- **Emptied memory blocks go back to the driver on a thread of their own,** not on the render thread: 4 to 7 ms a block on
  an RTX 3060 Ti, up to 250 ms on others, many in a row at area changes. `RX_VK_FREE_LATER=0`: as before. From upstream
  Hookshot (pixl27), via HeyListen.

## Kept
- The PC profile, Fog boxes (Off), Fog density and Brightness (100 %), the in-game diagnosis, the master log after every run,
  Update inside the builder, and the fixes of v0.3.8 to v0.3.13. The prepared shaders stay: nothing here changes the shader
  translation.

## Known issues
- Some places look too bright in a white haze (a fairy's fountain, the Zora's domain); the cause is open.
- Busy areas (the open field) drop below 30 fps now and then on handhelds.
